# synth.kicad_blocks

Reusable schematic design blocks for synth modules.

## Power Block

Eurorack power input, ready to drop into a module schematic.

- 10-pin Eurorack power connector
- 1N5819 Schottky diodes for reverse-polarity protection
- 10 Ω series resistors on the supply rails
- 47 µF / 16 V bulk capacitors and 0.1 µF decoupling capacitors
- Test points, plus `VCC`, `-VCC` and `GND` global labels to connect the rest of the circuit
